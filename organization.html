Thank you

Köpri exists thanks to the people who review code in Turkmen and Russian, open issues and believe that a small language deserves great software. This page is our small thank-you note.

WHAT WE ARE GRATEFUL FOR

Open code

MIT, Apache-2.0, GPL-3.0 — take it, change it, use it. Reviews happen in the open.

Works offline

No internet required. Internet is expensive and slow in Turkmenistan — this is a principle, not an accident.

Free & no ads

No ads, no payments, no data collection. Ever.

In Turkmen

Interfaces in Turkmen; code review in Turkmen and Russian — weak English is not a barrier.
