FEATURES

Everything you need in one app

Translation, phrasebook, learning and motivation — built offline-first with native performance.

Text-to-text translation

Instant translation between 50+ languages online, and Russian / English → Turkmen fully offline.

Offline-first

Phrasebook, history, favourites, XP and statistics work without internet. Your data stays on your device.

Camera translation (soon)

Hybrid OCR in v2.0.0: ML Kit for Latin (~0.3s) + Tesseract for Cyrillic, Arabic, CJK and Devanagari.

Huge phrasebook

4731 phrases in dozens of categories and subcategories — from greetings to restaurant and allergies.

Flashcards

Learn words with cards: quick review anywhere, even with no network around.

History & favourites

Every translation is saved locally. Pin the phrases you use often — they are always one tap away.

Profile & progress

XP levels 1–100, daily streaks, badges and the title “Translation Legend” for the most dedicated.

Native speed

Heavy calculations (XP, streaks, stats, JSON) run in C++17 via FFI — 10–60× faster than pure Dart.
